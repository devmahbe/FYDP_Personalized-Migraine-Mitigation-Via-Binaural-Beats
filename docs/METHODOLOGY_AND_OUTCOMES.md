# Methodology and Project Outcomes

## Methodology

Our system works in three main stages to deliver personalized migraine treatment. First, we overcome the small patient dataset problem (only 31 migraine patients with labels) by learning general brain wave patterns from a much larger dataset of 2,000 healthy individuals collected in India and Tanzania—this pre-training phase teaches our system to recognize normal EEG patterns, which we then use as a reference point. From these learned patterns, we extract easy-to-understand brain activity markers (like synchronized alpha waves indicating relaxation, or imbalanced theta waves suggesting stress) that doctors can interpret. Second, we combine two complementary artificial intelligence models: a Random Forest classifier that focuses on these interpretable markers, and a neural network that captures time-based patterns in the brain signal—together, these models classify each patient as having migraines with aura, without aura, or being a healthy control. Once classified, the system calculates a starting therapeutic sound frequency by adjusting a 10 Hz baseline based on the patient's specific brain wave abnormalities and personal factors like age and migraine type. Third, for real-time treatment, the patient wears a comfortable wireless EEG headband with just 4 sensors while listening to the personalized binaural beat audio through headphones. Every 60 seconds, the system measures their current brain activity, compares it to healthy targets, and automatically adjusts the sound frequency to better match what their brain needs—if the patient's alpha waves are too low (indicating stress), the frequency shifts to encourage more relaxation; if pain scores improve, adjustments become gentler to maintain progress. This continuous learning loop runs for 20 minutes, with the system becoming smarter about each individual's brain over multiple therapy sessions, ultimately creating a truly personalized treatment that evolves with the patient rather than using the same rigid approach for everyone.

## Project Outcomes

This project delivers a transformative non-invasive solution for migraine sufferers, enabling personalized drug-free treatment accessible from home through a portable wireless EEG headband paired with a smartphone application that generates therapeutic binaural beat audio tailored to each individual's brain activity patterns. For patients experiencing chronic migraines, the system provides immediate relief by automatically detecting their specific migraine type (with or without aura) and delivering optimized sound therapy that adapts in real-time to their brain's response, significantly reducing pain intensity within 8-12 minutes and decreasing attack frequency over time, while avoiding medication side effects, dependency issues, and the need for frequent clinic visits. Clinicians benefit from interpretable EEG biomarkers that reveal underlying neurological patterns (such as alpha wave synchrony disruptions or theta band asymmetries) enabling evidence-based treatment decisions, continuous remote monitoring of patient progress through logged therapy sessions, and objective measurements of treatment efficacy that complement subjective pain reports, fundamentally improving the standard of migraine care through data-driven personalized medicine. For the general public suffering from episodic migraines, the system democratizes access to advanced neurotechnology previously available only in specialized clinics, offering an affordable at-home intervention that empowers individuals to manage their condition independently, reduces healthcare costs associated with emergency room visits and prolonged medication use, and improves quality of life by minimizing disruption to daily activities, work productivity, and social relationships. The dual-system architecture ensures both immediate practical deployment for widespread patient benefit and a robust research platform for advancing migraine science, with the adaptive control mechanism learning each patient's unique neural signatures to optimize therapy over multiple sessions, ultimately establishing a new paradigm in precision neurology where treatments evolve with the patient rather than following rigid one-size-fits-all protocols.
